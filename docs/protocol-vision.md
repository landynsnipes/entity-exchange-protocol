# EXP protocol vision

EXP is a model-neutral protocol. An authorized assistant, application, or gateway can say what an
entity seeks or offers, then evaluate one counterpart from disclosed attributes, explicit
constraints, and evidence.

## The daily-driver AI relationship

A person's chosen AI may know preferences from long-term private interaction. That knowledge is
not itself an EXP record. For one user request, the AI may derive a minimal Intent Projection such
as "no visible logos, athletic fit, under USD 80." The projection identifies its purpose,
authorization, expiration, disclosed attributes, and omitted sensitive categories. It explicitly
cannot contain raw conversation content.

The person remains the principal. The AI is a delegated agent, not the owner of the person profile
or an independent source of consent. EXP authorization never grants a model access to another
model's memory; it only governs the structured projection intentionally placed into an exchange.

## Reciprocal discovery

Both sides can express intent. A consumer may seek a product. A provider may offer capabilities or
seek a consenting audience segment. Reciprocal discovery does not expose a list of named people to
advertisers. A gateway evaluates compatible, permissioned projections and proposes a connection or
offer to the appropriate principal.

## Protocol and gateways

The EXP foundation defines identity references, intent, delegated authority, discovery,
contextual evaluation, proposals, decisions, evidence, provenance, consent, and expiry. Domain
profiles define vocabulary. Gateways provide user experiences and integrations.

- Career Profile: people, organizations, and opportunities.
- Commerce Profile: consumer intent, products, and provider offers.
- Future profiles: travel, dining, services, relationships, procurement, care, and agent services.

No gateway is the protocol, and no central registry is required for conformance.
